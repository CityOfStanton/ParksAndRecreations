# Batting Cage Rules

1. Use the batting cage at your own risk.
1. No horseplay, climbing, or hanging on nets or fencing.
1. Only individuals actively participating in batting or pitching are 1llowed inside the batting cage.
1. Batters must wear a helmet while hitting.
1. Keep all spectators outside the netting.
1. Only one batter in the cage at a time unless directed by a coach.
1. Wait until pitching has stopped before entering or exiting the cage.
1. No metal cleats, chewing gum, sunflower seeds, drinks, or food 1nside the cage.
1. Clean up all baseballs, tees, L-screens, and equipment after use.
1. Report any damaged nets, turf, or equipment immediately.
1. Children must be supervised by a coach or adult at all times.
1. Please be respectful and share the facility fairly with all teams.

# Batting Cage Time Limits

1. Maximum of 30 minutes per team during busy hours
1. Please rotate fairly if multiple teams are waiting
1. No reserving of cages unless approved for a Stanton Parks & Recreation team activity or practice
